# FH Match Centre

FH Match Centre is a field hockey umpiring system. You run the match on your watch; the result, cards and timeline move to your phone by themselves, where you can check them, fix mistakes, send a match report or a red card report, and (from the beta) publish the result to the website.

It's made for club umpires: the watch does the timekeeping and remembers everything, so you can keep your eyes on the game.

## What's in it

- **A watch app** for Wear OS (Samsung Galaxy Watch 4 and later, Google Pixel Watch) and Apple Watch: the match clock, goals, green, yellow and red cards with suspension timers, shootouts, and vibrations you can tell apart without looking. It works with no phone and no signal.
- **A phone app** for Android and iPhone: every match you've umpired, editing, one-page match reports (PDF), spreadsheets (CSV), backups, red card reports and your workouts.
- **A website** (from the beta): publish results, share them with a link or QR code, and let clubs see their matches.
