# The Apple Watch app

The Apple Watch app works like the [Wear OS app](guide/wear-os.md): the same setup, the same four pages (Timing, Goals, Cards and Settings), the same rules for suspensions and the same vibrations. This page covers what's different.

?> The Apple Watch app is new in the beta, for watchOS 10 and later (Series 4 and newer).

## Starting and stopping the clock

Apple doesn't let apps use the side button or the Digital Crown press, so the **Timing** page has a **Start/Stop** button.

On a **Series 9, Ultra 2 or later** with watchOS 11, **double-tap** (tap your index finger and thumb together twice) presses it, without touching the screen.

## Keeping the match running

The first time you start a match, the watch asks for **Health** access. The app runs a workout while the match is on, which is what keeps the clock, suspension timers and vibrations going with your wrist down. Allow it; without it the match only runs while the app is on screen.

The workout is only kept (in the Health app, and sent to your phone) if you turn on **Record workout** in Settings. See [Workouts](guide/workouts.md).

## Setting up on the phone

The watch can't open the iPhone app for you. To set up a match on the phone, open the phone app, go to **Settings → Set up a match**, fill it in and tap **Send to watch** with the watch app open nearby. The setup opens on the watch to check and start.

## Syncing

Finished matches go to your iPhone automatically, even when you're out of range for a while: they're queued and delivered when the phone is back. The summary on the watch says **✓ On your phone** once the phone has confirmed it.

## Updates

The watch app comes inside the iPhone app, so it updates whenever the iPhone app does (through TestFlight in the beta).
