# Club umpiring

Clubs can organise their umpires here: keep a list of them, enter the fixtures they need umpires for, and ask umpires to take them. It's all extra. Umpires still set up and umpire any match themselves, for any club or none, exactly as before.

Each fixture has a **watch umpire**, who runs the watch app and gets the match set up on their phone, and, when the fixture needs two, a **second umpire**, who's named on the match. One watch per match keeps the timings straight.

## For umpires

Everything is under **Umpiring** on the website.

### Appointments

When a club asks you to umpire, you get an email. Open **Umpiring → Appointments** (or **Upcoming** in the phone app) and **Accept** or **Decline**. Either way the club's admins are told, so if you decline they can ask someone else.

- A match you accept as the **watch umpire** appears in the phone app's **Upcoming**, already set up with the teams, format, venue and competition. Add the colours and captains, and send it to the watch at the ground as usual. If the club changes the fixture before you've sent it, the change follows. If you delete it from Upcoming, it isn't added again.
- As **second umpire**, it's shown in **Upcoming** above your own matches, with who you're umpiring with.
- The phone reminds you at 6pm the day before each match you've accepted (if notifications are on, in **Settings**).
- If the club moves or cancels the fixture, you're emailed.

### Can't make it?

On **Appointments**, **Ask for cover**. The club's other umpires and its admins are emailed, and anyone on the club's list can **Take it over** from their own Appointments page. Until someone does, the match is still yours; **I can make it after all** stops asking.

### Availability

**Umpiring → Availability** shows the next 12 weeks. Tap a day once if you're free, twice if you're not, and again to unmark it. For a day you're only free for part of, set the hours below the calendar. **Never free on** marks weekdays you're never available, unless you mark a particular day otherwise.

Clubs see this only as "free", "not free" or "not said" for each fixture, when they choose who to ask.

### Your calendar

**Appointments** has a private address for your calendar: add it in Google, Apple or Outlook Calendar ("add calendar from URL", or "subscribe"), and the matches you accept appear there. Anyone with the address can see them, so keep it to yourself. **Make a new address** replaces it if it gets out.

### My season

**Umpiring → My season** counts the matches you've umpired and the appointments you've taken since 1 August, with a list of each.

## For club admins

The **Dashboard** links to your club's **fixtures** and **umpires**.

### The umpire list

**Club umpires**: find an umpire by name to add them. They need an account on the site, which anyone can make. For each, set their **level** (Trainee, Level 1 (Unassessed), Level 1 (Assessed), Level 2, Level 3 or National) and the **team they play for**: they won't be suggested for that team's matches, or ones at the same time. **This season** counts the appointments they've accepted for your club, so they can be shared out fairly. An umpire can be on several clubs' lists, and can leave one.

### Fixtures

**Club fixtures** lists your matches from today, with who's umpiring each and whether it **needs** more. **Short of umpires** shows only those.

**Add a fixture**: the date and kick-off, the teams, the venue and competition, the format (sent to the watch umpire's phone, or left to them), how many umpires it needs (one, when the other side provides one), and any notes. Your teams by name ("M1"), and other clubs' as club and team ("Reading M1"), are linked to the site's teams.

**Import** adds a season's fixtures at once from a spreadsheet saved as CSV, with the columns `date, time, home, away, venue, competition, umpires`. Only the date and teams are needed. Dates can be `26/09/2026` or `2026-09-26`, and times `14:00` or `2pm`. **Preview** shows what would be added and any rows with problems first; fixtures already there (the same day and teams) are left alone.

### Appointing

Open a fixture. **Who to ask** lists your club's umpires, best first:

1. Free, then not said, then not free that day (or outside the hours they gave).
2. Those with nothing against them before those with something, such as playing in the match or that day, umpiring at the same time, umpiring at another ground with little time to get there, or a level below what the competition asks for.
3. Whoever has done fewest this season.

Choose **watch** or **second umpire**, tick **Mentoring** to pair a newer umpire with an experienced one, and **Ask**. They're emailed, and their answer shows on the fixture; you're emailed when they accept or decline. **Take off** removes someone, and tells them.

Changing a fixture's date, kick-off or venue emails the umpires on it; deleting it tells them it's off.

### Emails about gaps

On Mondays, and two days before a fixture still short of umpires, your club's admins get an email listing the next week's fixtures that need someone, and who's been asked without answering yet.

## For admins

**Admin → Competitions** sets the lowest umpire level each competition asks for. Clubs see anyone below it flagged when they appoint, though they can still ask them.
