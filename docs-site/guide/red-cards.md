# Red card reports

After a red card, England Hockey needs a report through its online **Red Card and Misconduct Complaint Form**. The phone app gathers everything the form asks for, filled in from the match where it can be, so completing the form is a matter of copying and pasting.

## Starting a report

Open the match on the phone. A **Red card report** card at the top lists each red card, with where its report stands: **Not started**, **Started** or **Submitted**. Tap **Start** (or **Open**).

## What's filled in for you

| From the match | |
| --- | --- |
| **Shirt number**, **team** | From the card. |
| **Fixture** | "Home vs. Away". |
| **League and division** | The match's competition, if set. |
| **Date of match** | When the match started. |
| **Your colleague** | The other umpire, if the match lists two and your name is one of them. |
| **Details of offence** | Begins with the time and reason of the card (e.g. "Red card at Q3 4:12, physical misconduct") and the venue. |

## What you add

Following England Hockey's checklist:

1. **You**: your name, umpiring qualification and contact details. These are remembered for your next report.
2. **Your colleague**: their name.
3. **Offender and club**: the offender's name, whether they're over or under 18, and their club. The offender's club must give you these details. For a schools or representative match, the form's club list has **Other**.
4. **Competition and match**: the Area (East, Midlands, North, South or West), and check the league and fixture.
5. **Details of offence**: what happened, in your own words.

Everything saves as you type. A list at the bottom shows anything still to fill in.

## Sending it

- **Open England Hockey's form**: opens the online form.
- **Tap an answer to copy it**, then paste it into the matching question in the form. With split screen on a larger phone, you can see both at once.
- **Share as PDF**: the whole report on one page, to email to your Area Disciplinary Administrator or keep for your records.
- **Copy it all as text**: for an email or a note.
- **Mark as submitted**: once you've sent the form, so the match shows the report as done.

For how to complete the form, see the England Hockey website or your Area's, or ask your Area Disciplinary Administrator.

?> Reports stay on your phone (and in your backups). They're never uploaded to the website.
