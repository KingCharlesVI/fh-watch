On the pitch

Built for the umpire, not the scorer

Everything you need during a match, on the watch you already wear. Nothing you don't.

A match clock you can trust

Quarters or halves, any length, with breaks. Counts up or down, and keeps perfect time through stoppages, a flat screen or a restart.

Start and stop with a button

Press the watch's side button to stop and restart the clock, without looking. Works on Galaxy Watch 4 and later.

Cards and suspensions

Green, yellow and red cards with the player's number. Suspension timers pause when the clock does, and buzz when time's served.

Feel what's happening

Distinct vibrations for one minute left, time up, a suspension ending and the end of a break.

No signal needed

Everything works offline at the ground. The match moves to your phone by itself when the watch is next in reach.

The paperwork, done

Goals, corners, strokes and shootouts become a one-page match report, a spreadsheet or a backup, straight from your phone.

How it works

Watch, phone, website

  1. 01

    Umpire on the watch

    Start the match, record goals, cards and corners in a couple of taps. The clock never stops being accurate.

  2. 02

    Check it on your phone

    The finished match arrives on your phone. Fix any mistakes, add the umpires, and export the report.

  3. 03

    Publish it

    Coming in the beta: publish the result for players and clubs, and share it by link or QR code.
